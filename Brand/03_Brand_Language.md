# ENERGYTWIN AI — BRAND LANGUAGE
### Voice, Tone, Vocabulary & Messaging System (Arabic-First, English-Ready)
---

## 1. Voice Definition

**We sound like: a financially-smart, warm Egyptian friend who happens to know everything about solar — and can't wait to tell you how much you'll save.**

Three voice pillars:
1. **صريح (Straight-talking)** — no fluff, no jargon, numbers up front
2. **دافئ (Warm)** — Egyptian warmth, greetings, encouragement
3. **واثق (Confident)** — we know Egyptian tariffs cold; we never hedge weakly

## 3. Language Rules

### Golden Rule: Money First, Planet Second
- ✅ Lead with savings, ROI, payback → then mention clean energy as the proud bonus
- ❌ Never open with climate/SDG language to shop owners — that comes *after* the money hook
- Exception: for Gen Z/startup content, sustainability and independence can share the lead — A/B test this

### Jargon Translation Table

| Never say (jargon) | Say instead |
|---|---|
| kWp / كيلووات قمية | "قدرة النظام" + plain number, e.g. "نظام ٢٠ كيلووات" |
| System capacity / inverter | "الألواح اللي سقفك ياخدها" |
| Photovoltaic | "ألواح شمسية" — always |
| Payback period | "هتسترد فلوسك في X سنين وبعدها الكهربا ببلاش" |
| Net metering | "الفائض بيتحسب لصالحك" |
| Load profile | "استهلاكك الحالي" |
| Green Micro-Finance | "تمويل بنكي أخضر / قرض للطاقة الشمسية" |

### Do / Don't Examples

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |

## 4. Signature Phrases (Brand Vocabulary)

### Arabic (Egyptian)
- **"خلّي الشمس تدفع الفاتورة"** — *Let the sun pay the bill* (campaign line)
- **"صوّر الفاتورة… وشوف الفرق"** — *Photograph the bill… see the difference*
- **"سقفك بيضيع. خليه يوفر."** — *Your roof is going to waste. Make it save.*
- **"هتسترد فلوسك في X سنين، وبعدها؟ كهربا شبه ببلاش"**
- **"الحساب بالمصري: فاتورتك × تعريفة تجاري × ساعات شمس مصر"** — *The Egyptian math*
- **"استقلالك الكهربائي يبدأ من فاتورة"** — for the independence angle (younger audience)

### English
- "Your bill, your roof, your savings — in 30 seconds."
- "Solar, sized for the corner shop."
- "From bill photo to bank-ready plan."
- "Own your power."

### Words we love
وَفّر / استرد / سقفك / ٣٠ ثانية / بلاش / ببساطة / بنفسك / مضمون

### Words we avoid
شراء عاجل / عرض محدود / فرصة ذهبية / فلاش (scam-sounding) / اضغط هنا (spammy) / any fear-mongering

## 5. Messaging Framework by Audience

### For the shop owner (30–48)
> **Hook:** فاتورة الكهربا طولت وزادت؟
> **Problem:** التعريفة التجارية بتزيد كل سنة، وأنت بتدفع زيادة من غير ما تحس.
> **Solution:** صوّر الفاتورة من موبايلك. في ٣٠ ثانية نحسبلك: نظام بكام؟ هتوفر كام؟ وهتسترد فلوسك امتى؟ — وكمان تقرير جاهز للبنك لو حبيت تمويل.
> **Proof:** أرقام حقيقية من فاتورتك، بتعريفات مصر التجارية الرسمية.

### For the startup founder / Gen Z
> **Hook:** Burn rate got a line item you haven't optimized: the roof.
> **Problem:** Your energy costs eat your runway, and "going green" usually costs more, not less.
> **Solution:** AI reads your bill and your roof from space. Get exact EGP savings, ROI, and a bank-ready green finance report — investor-deck material in one click.
> **Proof:** Real Egyptian tariff data, real satellite roof measurement.

## 6. Bilingual & RTL Rules

1. **Arabic is the default interface; English is one tap away** (header toggle: ع | EN)
2. Arabic UI = full RTL: mirrored layouts, icons with direction (arrows, progress) flipped
3. Numbers: Eastern Arabic numerals in Arabic UI; Western in reports
4. Never machine-translate product copy — the Egyptian dialect tone must be written by a human
5. Brand name stays "EnergyTwin AI" in both languages
6. Mixed-script lines: Arabic words + Latin numbers — keep number direction consistent (use `direction: ltr` on numeric spans)
